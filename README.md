## Current projects

### [Arynwood MCP](https://github.com/Arynwood-Technology/ArynwoodMCP)

[![CI](https://github.com/Arynwood-Technology/ArynwoodMCP/actions/workflows/ci.yml/badge.svg)](https://github.com/Arynwood-Technology/ArynwoodMCP/actions/workflows/ci.yml)

A personal AI and creative workspace that runs on your own machine: chat with local models through Ollama, search and remember your own documents, design on a canvas, work with music and video tools, and open your Community spaces, all in one desktop app.

**Where it stands:** v0.5.5 desktop alpha, published November 3, 2026, for Linux x86_64 (AppImage and .deb) and Windows x64. Releases from 0.5.4 on have owner-signed checksums and verified build provenance.

### [Arynwood Grove](https://github.com/Arynwood-Technology/arynwood-community)

[![CI](https://github.com/Arynwood-Technology/arynwood-community/actions/workflows/ci.yml/badge.svg)](https://github.com/Arynwood-Technology/arynwood-community/actions/workflows/ci.yml)
[![OpenSSF Scorecard](https://api.scorecard.dev/projects/github.com/Arynwood-Technology/arynwood-community/badge)](https://scorecard.dev/viewer/?uri=github.com/Arynwood-Technology/arynwood-community)

Private community software for the people in your life: shared calendars, tasks, lists, notes, files and discussion, by invitation, on a server you control. Arynwood MCP's Community page opens a Grove inside the desktop app.

**Where it stands:** v0.7.5, published November 3, 2026. A one-command installer sets it up on an Ubuntu or Debian server, or it runs on your own Linux or macOS computer. [community.arynwood.com](https://community.arynwood.com) runs on it.

[Website](https://arynwood.com/community/) · [Download](https://github.com/Arynwood-Technology/arynwood-community/releases/latest) · [Install guide](https://github.com/Arynwood-Technology/arynwood-community/blob/main/deploy/README.md)
